## 🎯 Core Innovation: Fairness Metrics

FairCoin.app Indonesia introduces three revolutionary fairness indexes:

### **1. PFI★ (Personal Fairness Index)**
- Measures individual contributions to the community (0-100 scale)
- Earned through verified acts of service, code contributions, and positive behavior
- Determines voting power and resource distribution eligibility
- **Cannot be bought**—only earned through genuine contribution

### **2. TFI★ (Trade Fairness Index)**
- Measures merchant/trader quality and ethics (0-100 scale)
- Based on delivery success, customer satisfaction, price transparency, environmental impact
- Protects consumers from exploitation and fraud
- Rewards ethical business practices

### **3. CBI★ (Community Fairness Index)**
- Aggregated measure of entire community health
- Guides monetary policy and resource distribution
- Ensures system stability anchored to real economic activity
- Prevents speculation and manipulation
