## ✨ **THE SOLUTION: FairCoin.app Indonesia**

**An open-source, community-driven transaction system that rewards fairness over wealth concentration.**

### **Three Revolutionary Fairness Metrics:**

#### **PFI★ (Personal Fairness Index)**
📊 **0-100 score** based on community contributions  
✅ Earned through service, not purchased with money  
🗳️ Determines voting power and resource distribution  

#### **TFI★ (Trade Fairness Index)**
⭐ **Merchant quality score** based on ethics  
🛡️ Protects consumers from fraud and exploitation  
🌱 Rewards transparency and environmental responsibility  

#### **CBI★ (Community Fairness Index)**
🌍 **Aggregate community health metric**  
📈 Guides monetary policy and stability  
🔒 Prevents speculation and manipulation
