# FAIRCOIN COMMUNITY BUDGET FLOW RULES

(Version 1.0 — Non-Profit, Score-Based Social Fairness System)

---

## 1. Nature of Faircoin

Faircoin is not money. It is not a deposit system, not a crowdfunding platform, and not a payment instrument.

Faircoin is a non-profit social fairness engine (paguyuban / ormas).

Its crypto wallet only stores PFI★ scores, not financial assets.

## 2. No Financial Handling Inside Faircoin

Faircoin:

- ❌ does not receive money.
- ❌ does not store money.
- ❌ does not process deposits or withdrawals.
- ❌ does not touch the CSR or PT funds.

All money stays 100% inside each organisation’s own PT / CSR system.

Faircoin only provides:

- Score distribution logic
- Fairness percentage calculation
- Permanent on-chain PFI★ records
- Transparent auto-allocation rules

## 3. Wallet Types

A. Personal Wallet (Volunteer / Member)

- Stores their PFI score.
- Receives score bonuses after social work.
- Free to use, no deposit required.

B. Faircoin Treasury Wallet

- Holds percentage share, not money.
- Reflects each member’s fairness ratio for future proposals.

C. External PT Wallets (Separate Legal Entity)

- Owned by organisations such as PTs, companies, or donors (e.g., donor organisations or companies).
- They hold real funds; these are separate from Faircoin.

## 4. Flow of Funds (Clear Separation)

A. Donor → PT Funding Account

Example:

A donor donates Rp 1.000.000 to fix a street. This money goes into the donor organization's CSR account — not Faircoin.

B. PT Converts Internal Budget → PFI Score

Conversion example:

Rp 1.000.000 = 100 PFI score (no money enters Faircoin)

C. Faircoin Records the Scores

Volunteers earn PFI based on their contribution.

Example:

- 10 people bring water → each gets 1 PFI
- 20 people bring food → each gets 1 PFI
- Remaining PFI stays with the donor organization (70)

D. PT Releases Actual Bonus

The PT later releases the 30% volunteer bonus directly:

PT → Volunteers (via bank, e-wallet, crypto, etc.)

Faircoin only confirms who earned what, based on the on-chain PFI logs.

## 5. Example Cycle (Complete Scenario)

1. Donor gives: Rp 1.000.000 — stored entirely in PT’s account.
2. PT calculates PFI: 100 and registers it into Faircoin as:

   - Donor organization: 70
   - 10 water contributors: 10
   - 20 food contributors: 20

3. Community Action

   - 200 people gather.
   - 30 people contribute.
   - 170 people are inactive → receive no PFI.

4. Bonus Distribution

   PT releases 30% (Rp 300.000) to the 30 active volunteers.

   Distribution fairness is guaranteed by PFI × total budget formula.

   Example calculation:

   - Volunteer with 1 PFI from the donor organization receives: 1/100 × Rp 1.000.000 = Rp 10.000

   Reminder: Money comes from PT; score comes from Faircoin. No mixing.

## 6. Why This Is Legally Safe

Faircoin does not run financial services.

- Faircoin does not collect deposits.
- Faircoin does not manage money.

Faircoin provides only:

- Transparency
- Social fairness logs
- Volunteer scoring
- Anti-korupsi allocation

As notaris confirmed: Paguyuban / ormas status is sufficient.

## 7. Compliance Notes

- Any funds donated personally to individuals (bank/ETH) = third-party handling, subject to normal personal income tax.
- Any ads revenue goes to third-party accounts, not Faircoin.
- PT distributes bonus directly to volunteers, outside the Faircoin system.

## 8. Essence of Faircoin

Faircoin is:

- A fairness oracle, not a bank.
- A volunteer honor ledger, not a crowdfunding tool.
- A social trust engine, not a financial institution.
- A score-based anti-corruption mechanism for communities.

PFI is:

- A permanent fairness score
- Stored on-chain
- Used for future proposals
- A small appreciation bonus for social-good workers
