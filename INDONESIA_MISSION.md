## ✨ Background & Spiritual Origin

The idea of Faircoin.app came not from reading or studying existing "fair" projects,  
but through a **spiritual conviction** — that the Creator desires fairness  
to be measurable, visible, and teachable.

It was inspired by witnessing injustice, poverty, and social imbalance —  
where those who try to do good often remain unheard.  
In this silence, came the realization that **divine fairness**  
is not about human power or profit, but about **right relationship** —  
between people, creation, and God.

This became the seed for:

- **PFI★ (Personal Fairness Index)** — measuring fairness of heart, action, and word.
- **TFI★ (Trade Fairness Index)** — encouraging honesty and equity in exchange.
- **CFI★ (Community Fairness Index)** — nurturing fair governance and peace.

## ⚠️ Important Clarification: No Affiliation with European FairChain/Faircoin

**Faircoin.app Indonesia is an independent project** and is **NOT officially affiliated** with:

- The European FairChain Foundation
- The European Faircoin cryptocurrency project
- Any other "FairCoin" or "FairChain" initiatives worldwide

### Our Independent Identity

While we share similar values of fairness and justice, our project:

- Was **independently conceived** through spiritual inspiration
- Has **different technical foundations** (not a cryptocurrency)
- Serves **different purposes** (ethical measurement, not financial trading)
- Operates **independently** from any existing fair-economy movements

If in the future there is an opportunity to connect or collaborate with European FairChain,  
the Faircoin community, or other fair-economy initiatives worldwide,  
**this project would be open to such possibilities**.

We respect and honor all who work toward fairness and justice,  
and believe that collaboration in the spirit of peace is always welcome.

However, as of now, we operate **independently** and make no claim to affiliation  
with any existing projects or organizations.
