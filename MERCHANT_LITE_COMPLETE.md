```
1. Browse Merchants (/lite/merchants)
   ↓
2. Click "Become a Merchant"
   ↓
3. Step 1: Business & Owner Info
   ↓
4. Step 2: Category & Contact
   ↓
5. Step 3: Description & Submit
   ↓

   ↓

```
